#Advanced #Partisan #Flanking
 
- Performance Time: Attack action
* Required Skill: Intimidation (adept) or Performance (adept)
* DC Penalty: -1
 
If either you or a flanking ally have taken damage during the previous round, you may leverage that adrenaline to retaliate, infusing you both with terrifying motivation. You get a +1 bonus to your damage rolls. On a hit, each successive hitting attack from either you or the ally get the same stacking bonus for hitting the flanked creature until the start of your next turn. On a miss, the effect ends.  
At the start of your next turn, both creatures recover hit points equal to the amount of successful attacks during this technique's effect.
