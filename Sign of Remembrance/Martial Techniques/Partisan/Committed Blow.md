#Basic #Partisan #Movement #Time #Armed
 
- Performance Time: Attack action and quick action
* Required Skill: Athletics (proficient)
* DC Penalty: +2
- Movement Cost: 25'
- Required Weapon: Any heavy weapon
 
You put as much power as you can into one decisive attack, attempting to blow away your opponent. Your attack gains the Lethal 2 property and deals 1 additional damage.
