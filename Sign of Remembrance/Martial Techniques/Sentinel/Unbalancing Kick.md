#Basic #Sentinel #Armed
 
- Performance Time: [[Action Economy#^5d5dd0|Shove]] action
* Required Skill: Intimidation (proficient)
* DC Penalty: +2
- Required Weapon: Unarmed strike (legs)

You perform a powerful shove with your leg, throwing them backward. If the enemy fails their saving throw including your penalty, the shove throws them back 5' further than a usual shove action. This is considered an unarmed strike, which means it considered a [[Weapon Rules#Light|light weight]].
