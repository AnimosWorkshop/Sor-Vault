#Advanced #Fighter #Armed
 
- Performance Time: 1 reaction to an activated Guard
* Required Skill: Athletics (adept) and Sleight of Hand (adept)
* DC Penalty: -
- Required Weapon: A non-heavy and light weapons, one in each hand, or one weapon and a shield if you have the [[Fighter#Dirty Tactics|Shield Bash]] dirty tactic
 
When your guard is via from the [[Fighter#On Guard|on guard]] feature, if the attack made against you is a melee weapon attack, you attempt to disarm the wielder entirely instead of blocking the attack. The attacker must make a Resilience save against the skill you chose, dropping the weapon 5' away on a failure. On a success, the attack's damage is reduced by half, before the guard's damage reduction.
