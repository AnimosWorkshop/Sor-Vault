#Basic #Fighter #Flanking #Time
 
- Performance Time: 1 attack and quick action
* Required Skill: Intimidation (proficient)
* DC Penalty: +2

You utilize the momentum of your blow and your foe's poor circumstances to strike fear into their heart. You also use the [[Action Economy#^362ce9|demoralize]] quick action if they are within its range. They make their saving throw against it with a -2 penalty.
