#Basic #Fighter #Armed
 
- Performance Time: Quick action
* Required Skill: Athletics (proficient)
* DC Penalty: +2
- Required Weapon: Hook
 
You use your hook to expose your opponent to incoming attacks. You make a weapon attack with your hook as a quick action, meaning you do not receive an attribute modifier bonus to damage rolls, and you cannot [[Action Economy#^43d1b5|attack]] with a heavy weapon as your action.
If you hit, any attacks you make against the hooked creature until the end of your turn have a +1 bonus to the damage rolls and the Lethal 1 trait.
