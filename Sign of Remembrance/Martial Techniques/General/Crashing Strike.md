- Performance Time: Action and quick action
* Required Skill: Athletics (proficient) or Acrobatics (proficient)
* DC Penalty: +2
- Required Weapon: A melee weapon

You orient your weapon preemptively as you perform a [[Action Economy#^f8a635|dash action]], crashing into an opponent with your weapon. Run in a line deviating no more than 45$\degree$ every 20' of distance. In the end of your movement, you may make a weapon attack against your opponent, without adding your ability modifier to the damage roll. The damage die for this attack is reduced by 1 than the weapon's standard die, but its size is increased by 1 for every 20' of movement you passed.
