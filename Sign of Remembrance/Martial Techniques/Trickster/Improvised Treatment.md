#Basic #Trickster #Time
 
- Performance Time: Action
* Required Skill: Medicine (proficient)
* DC Penalty: +2
- Feature Required: [[Trickster#Field Surgeon|Field Surgeon specialty]]
 
Using improvised tools in the middle of the battlefield, you are able to extend the [[Action Economy#^79c113|medicate]] quick action to enhance your treatment. If you succeed on the technique, the amount of death save successes the creature gains is increased by 1.
