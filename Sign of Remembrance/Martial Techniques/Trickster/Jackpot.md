#Advanced #Trickster #Movement
 
- Performance Time: 1 attack
* Required Skill: Acrobatics (adept)
* DC Penalty: +5
- Movement Cost: 5'
 
Extending your attack quick action to an action against an enemy at the same combat initiative as you, you move in tandem with them to close the gap perfectly and land your hit. If you succeed on the technique, you critically hit.
